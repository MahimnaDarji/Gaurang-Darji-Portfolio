The Species That Endanger India

When the word "species" comes to mind, we typically think of our country's rich flora and fauna. In the race for rapid development, humanity has lost its sensitivity toward nature, driving many organisms to the brink of extinction as 'endangered' species. However, there is another kind of species capable of endangering an entire country or community. Living among us and eroding our society like termites, these are the individuals who refuse to follow the law of the land. To protect our country, it is vital to identify them. Through humor and satire, I would like to adopt the role of a palaeontologist and introduce you to the specific species that endanger India.

Books

Explore the books of Gaurang Darji across personal growth, communication, fiction, social commentary, humour and satire.

01

The First Day

The First Day stands as a testament to what young people, whose minds pulse with the power of 1000 TB processors, can achieve. This book teaches that a stone becomes a statue only by enduring the pain of the chisel, and a metal becomes an ornament only by withstanding intense heat without melting away. Every obstacle is an opportunity to take a leap. If you succeed, you move closer to your goal. The most crucial aspect of navigating the process of friction is simply enduring those difficult times. Only by surviving this process of friction will you define your shape, realise your true value and attain your goal.

02

Moneybhai the Magician

The Rupee's humorous roller-coaster ride begins with Moneybhai, the magician who captivates people's hearts, minds and pockets with his magic, along with his ardent fans. Seated on this ride racing along the tracks of money are quack doctors and bankers stuffing their bags, government bureaucrats and cultured godmen, the playful hero and the earnest social worker, the causes of inflation and politics that sink the nation, the flare-up of breaking news and the crash of the stock market. Come along and enjoy this humorous roller-coaster ride of money, one that surges with waves of laughter and cuts deep with sharp satire.

03

Parevaan

What is love? When two young hearts begin to love each other and dream of lifelong companionship, the first question that naturally arises is: What is love? Many remain so engrossed in seeking the answer that they fail to find love itself, while others do not concern themselves with such questions because their lives embody the definition of love. Hearts of various ages, those who have found love, lost it or yearn for it, reveal the true nature of love. From the perspective of our culture, love is a social bond that gains acceptance and respect through marriage. The answer lies in the seven vows of marriage. To discover it, read the love story of Revant and Vama.

04

Smart Learning

Learning and acquiring new knowledge is a fundamental human process. From the moment of birth until one's final breath, a person is constantly learning. Age is never a barrier to learning; the deciding factor is one's own interest. In the life of an ordinary person, learning proceeds in full swing from birth until the age of twenty-five, driven by necessity. After that, it becomes dependent on individual needs, goals and ambitions. A person who understands their goal can ensure that learning produces the desired results. This book is for inquisitive individuals who wish to keep learning, apply their knowledge and achieve the results they envision.

05

Communication is Life

Why a book on communication? It is a subject as simple as breathing, and everyone engages in it while going about daily life. Breathing is an inherent attribute of the body that can be controlled to some extent. Similarly, communication and dialogue are often treated as spontaneous bodily functions that require no conscious effort. That is a major mistake. Apart from breath itself, everything a human being needs, including water, food, love, emotions, relationships, friendship, encouragement and money, cannot be obtained without communication. Rather than viewing it merely as an inherent trait, we should cultivate it as a skill and an art form.

06

The Species That Endanger India

When the word species comes to mind, we typically think of our country's rich flora and fauna. In the race for rapid development, humanity has lost its sensitivity toward nature, driving many organisms to the brink of extinction as endangered species. However, there is another kind of species capable of endangering an entire country or community. Living among us and eroding society like termites, these are individuals who refuse to follow the law of the land. To protect the country, it is vital to identify them. Through humour and satire, the author adopts the role of a palaeontologist and introduces the specific species that endanger India.

07

Oxygen

Oxygen is colourless, odourless and tasteless, yet life is impossible without it. Scientifically, it is essential for cell growth, forms part of DNA, enables photosynthesis and helps create the ozone layer that protects life from ultraviolet rays. Most importantly, it is indispensable for respiration, which is why it is called Pranvayu, the vital breath of life. This book, however, is not about the gas. It is about people who bring positive change through noble deeds, compassionate behaviour, love and service. Such individuals breathe life into humanity and sustain the very breath of society.
